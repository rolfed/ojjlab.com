Why We Exist

We believe jiu jitsu makes our society better. Through our programs for ages 5 and up, we teach more than technique — we develop character. Every student learns humility in defeat, confidence through growth, perseverance under pressure, and problem-solving in real time.

Our approach challenges students both physically and mentally. We don't just build stronger bodies; we forge resilient minds that thrive under adversity and treat others with respect.

Our curriculum is built on constraint-based learning, where students train in real situations with purpose and pressure. Instead of memorizing moves they'll forget, they develop skills that show up when it matters — on the mats, in competition, and in life.

Our Core Values

Excellence in Instruction

Our instructors are dedicated to helping every student reach their potential through expert guidance, proven methods, and personal attention.

Our Training Approach

At Oregon Jiu Jitsu Lab, we use constraint-based learning to help students develop practical skills that work under pressure. Rather than drilling endless repetitions of techniques in isolation, we train in realistic scenarios where students must solve problems in real time.

This approach develops better problem-solvers, more confident competitors, and martial artists who can adapt to any situation. Students learn to think critically, stay calm under pressure, and execute techniques when it matters most.
